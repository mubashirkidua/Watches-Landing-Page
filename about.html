Muhammad Mubashir Ali

Founder & CEO

Muhammad Mubashir Ali founded The Ocean Watches with a conviction that luxury should be honest — built on trust, not just price tags. Under his leadership, the house grew from a single inspired sketch into a collection worn by over a thousand collectors across the world.

A relentless advocate for innovation, he pairs Swiss-grade engineering with ocean-born design language. But his deepest passion remains craftsmanship — personally reviewing every prototype, visiting every workshop, and answering every customer personally on WhatsApp.

Mubashir believes a brand is only as timeless as the trust it earns. That is why each Ocean watch carries his promise: uncompromising quality, lifetime care, and a relationship that never expires.
